# Toy brief (for a dry run)

A canned answer to question zero, and to round one's three picks. Generic on purpose: a pretend project, not a real one.

## Question zero: describe it in your own words

> me and my partner can never decide what to cook. i want a little thing where we both drop in dinner ideas during the week, each of us can give a thumbs up, and on sunday it shows the three with the most thumbs so we pick meals and make one shopping list. nothing fancy, i just don't want it to be a spreadsheet. we'd use it on our phones in the kitchen mostly.

Name for the project: `weeknight`. Its people: "cook" (singular), two of them.

## Round one's picks

1. **Who uses it?** Two people, both known, no strangers. (Pick: **a few people I know (sign-in, shared data)**.)
2. **Where does it live?** On the web, opened on phones, no app store. (Pick: **phone (a web app on the home screen)**.)
3. **How big?** Small: one release, a handful of screens. (Pick: **small**.)

## Follow-up facts the dry run may need

- Taste picks, one reason each:
  - dark-first: "we cook in the evening and it's a dim kitchen"
  - roomy: "big thumbs, my hands are wet"
  - playful copy: "it should feel like a kitchen note, not a form"
- App whose look you'd steal: a notes app with big, calm type · one you'd never: a busy grocery-delivery app.
- Cost ceiling: nothing per month. Data: first names and dinner ideas, two people.
- When to park it: "if we stop opening it for a month", "if one of us stops using it".
